日常の記録

things i keep close.

This page is a record of the three things I usually carry to class. Nothing special on their own. Put together, they feel like a small portrait of an ordinary school day.

01 / 03 小さな記録

saratoga

A shade of blue I always recognize.

I carry this blue Saratoga glass bottle to stay hydrated throughout the day. I like how familiar it looks on my desk, even when everything around it feels busy.

02 / 03 手のひらの道具

mx ergo

A little more control, a little less movement.

My MX Ergo is the mouse I bring for coding, editing, and design work. I like my stickers.

[ TRACKBALL · WORK / STUDY ]

03 / 03 考える場所

macbook

Somewhere to make things, or just think.

My MacBook is where most of my day happens: assignments, websites, ideas, research, and unfinished projects. The stickers make it feel a little more like mine.
